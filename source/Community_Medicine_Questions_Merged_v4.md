**Source:** Matrix 3rd Prof, 18th Ed., July 2026 — pages 149–177

## How this file was built
- **Exact repeats** are shown once.
- **Similar wording with practically the same answer** (e.g., "Define / Classify / Enumerate…", "Mention / Enlist / List…") are merged into one question, using the most complete wording.
- **Years:** all exam years from every merged version are combined, newest first. The `DU-` prefix is dropped from each entry. Session codes are as printed in the book: `Ja`, `M`, `Ju`, `S`, `Nov` (the book writes both `N` and `Nov`; both are shown here as `Nov`). Example: `24M` = DU-24M.
- **Stars:** the highest star rating among the merged versions is kept (★★★ / ★★ / ★; none = unmarked).
- **Calculation questions** with different data sets are kept separate, because their answers differ. Identical data sets are merged.
- **Case-based questions (SEQ / PBQ)** are kept in full.
- A question that appeared under two different headings is listed once, under the most relevant heading. See "Cross-heading merges" at the end of the file.
- **College papers added (v2):** questions from 11 term/assessment papers (DMC, SSMC, ShSMC, MMC, MuMC) were merged in using the same rules. Each merged entry now carries a `Colleges:` tag after the years.
- **College tag format:** `College (Paper Group+Q)`, e.g. `DMC (1st Assess A1)` = DMC 1st Assessment, Group A, Q1. Papers: DMC 1st/2nd Assess; SSMC 1st Supp / 2nd Term; ShSMC 1st Term / 2nd Term; MMC 1st Supp / 2nd Term; MuMC 1st Final / 1st Supp / 2nd Final.
- **Stars:** DU stars are kept as they were. Questions that had no DU star but appear in the college papers got a star by repeat count: ★★★ = asked by 3+ colleges (or 2 colleges in 4+ papers); ★★ = asked by 2 colleges (or 3+ different papers); ★ = repeated in 2 papers of one college. A question asked once only is left unstarred. MuMC 1st Final and 1st Supp papers repeat each other, so they count as one. A question with no `— *years*` is college-only.
- **Compound college questions** (e.g., "Define X. Describe Y.") were split into their parts and each part merged with the matching entry, or added as a new entry. When a college paper phrased a part differently, the `*(…)*` note after the tag shows the paper’s own wording.
- **Audit (v4):** the file was re-checked against book pp. 149–177. The DU questions of the communicable disease, NCD, geriatric, health administration and "Others" sections (pp. 171–177) had been left out of v3 (only their college-paper questions were present). All are now added in PART 11 and merged with the college tags; missing DU years were added to existing entries (UH&FPO, integrity & accountability, triage, offensive trades).
- **Group stars (v4 check):** in the book one star mark covers every wording printed under the same item number. A question that stays as its own entry (not merged) but sits under a starred number therefore carries that star too (e.g., primordial prevention under "levels of prevention").
- **Case-based college questions** are kept in full under the most relevant heading (see `#### Case-based questions (college papers)`). New sections for communicable diseases, NCDs and health administration are in PART 11.
