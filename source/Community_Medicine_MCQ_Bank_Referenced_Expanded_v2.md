## How this file was built

- **Source:** *Matrix 3rd Prof (18th Ed., July 2026)*, "MCQ of Community Medicine", pages 188–208 (21 pages). The pages are scanned images; every question, option and key was read from them one by one. Nothing was guessed: where the key is blank or unreadable it says **answer not given**.
- **Sessions found (14 papers × 20 MCQs):** 26M (May 2026), 25Nov, 25M, 24Nov, 24M, 23Nov, 23M, 22Nov-A, 22Nov-B, 22M, 21M (held Oct 2021), 20M&Nov (May & Nov 2020, held Mar 2021), 20Ju (July 2020, held Jan 2021), 19Nov. The “DU-” prefix is dropped. The PDF has **two** papers both headed “DU – November, 2022”; they are tagged **22Nov-A** (first) and **22Nov-B** (second).
- **Question types:** 20 SBA (26M Q11–20, 25Nov Q11–20) and 260 True/False stems (5 statements each, a–e).
- **Headings:** your order and names were used exactly; **no new headings were created**. Empty headings are kept and marked “No MCQs in this PDF”. Under each topic, SBA and True/False are separate sub-sections.
- **Merging rules applied**
  - *SBA:* no repeats exist in the PDF, so none merged.
  - *True/False:* each **statement** is the unit. All appearances of the same question stem (e.g. “Greenhouse gases are”) are gathered into one entry; inside it, statements are merged only when the statement **and** its T/F answer are the same, with the most complete wording kept. If the same statement appears with opposite answers, **both lines are kept and marked ⚠**. “Exclude / not / except” stems are kept as separate entries from their “include” twins because their answers are inverted.
  - Years are combined, newest first. A year followed by **†** means the key for that paper was hard to read on the scan (see Needs review).
- **Stars** (counted by number of appearances): ★★★ = 5+ appearances · ★★ = 3–4 · ★ = 2 · none = once. The stem line is starred by how often the stem appeared; each statement line is starred by how often that exact statement + answer appeared. (Your rule also says “3+ different sessions = ★★★”; that conflicts with “3–4 = ★★”, so I used the appearance counts above. Tell me if you want 3 sessions = ★★★ instead.)
- **Format:** `N. ★★★ Question — *years*`. SBA: options a–d with the correct one marked ✔. True/False: statements listed under the stem with their key.
